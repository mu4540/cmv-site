VFI-CG80 Series 220V 3HP(Brake) Vector Servo Drive

The VFI-CG80 vector servo drive is a high-performance inverter with four control modes: open-loop vector, flux vector, V/f control and V/f control with PG feedback. The control method is selected on the operator keypad to suit the application. A wide range of ratings is available to match customer needs.
