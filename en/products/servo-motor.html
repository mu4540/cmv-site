AC Servo Motor

SVMF and SVMJ AC servo vector induction motors in IP54 and IP23, with high torque at low speed.

The AC servo vector motor is an AC induction motor that delivers high torque at low speed. Its electrical characteristics keep performance high at both low and high speed, and the precise mechanical design improves stability and service life. Constant torque output meets the needs of high-precision motion control. Response to impact load is improved, so cutting performance rises clearly with either belt drive or direct coupling. Low power consumption and a low-noise design; a high-quality cast-iron frame with reinforced insulation extends service life. The motor holds constant horsepower at high speed and a stable magnetic field at low speed. It runs across the whole frequency range, delivers rated torque at zero speed, and accelerates and decelerates quickly. Compact size, with lower cost and energy use.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between IP54 and IP23?

IP54 is protected against dust and water splashing from any direction, for dusty environments. IP23 is protected against solid objects over 12.5 mm and sprayed water, for cleaner, drier locations. Choose by the installation environment.

Are dimension drawings available?

Yes. Each frame size page has a dimension drawing (in mm) and a specification table for each rated speed.

How do I get a price for a model?

Open the model page and choose "Ask for a price", or send the product code and quantity by Zalo. CMV replies with a quotation within business hours. Prices are not listed on the website.

What about lead time and warranty?

Lead time and warranty terms are stated in the quotation.
