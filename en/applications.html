Applications

Charger Mechatronic servo, drive and control systems run coil-processing lines, from a single shear to a whole plant.

Whole-plant automation lines

Control system design and integration for complete continuous strip-processing lines.

Cut-to-length (stop shear) line

A well-levelled shear line that delivers thick plate with excellent flatness.

Slitting line

High-precision slitting with inverter control systems.

Flying shear (rotary shear)

Fully closed-loop length measurement with a high-speed controller for precise positioning.

Tube cutting and bending

Controls for tube cut-off machines and all-electric or electro-hydraulic CNC/NC tube benders.

Other applications

Other coil-processing lines: coating, accumulators and bridle-roll drives.

Rotary shear retrofit

New controller, servo, drives and length encoder; sound mechanical parts stay.
