Flying shear (rotary shear)

Control system for flying shears (rotary shears): cuts sheet, coated steel and purlins while the line keeps running. A measuring roll reads length in closed loop and the high-speed CG controller synchronizes the knife with the material speed.

Who it is for

  • Roll-forming and C/Z purlin machine builders who need flying-cut control
  • Sheet and coated-steel plants running non-stop cutting lines
  • New lines that need a flying shear controller with technical support in Vietnam

What to send for a quote

  • Line speed (m/min)
  • Material, thickness and width
  • Typical cut lengths, current and target tolerance
  • Existing controller, motors and drives (if any)
  • Photos or video of the shear running

Frequently asked questions

Does a running flying shear need to be replaced entirely?

Not necessarily. If the mechanical parts are sound, CMV replaces only the controls and drive. See the rotary shear retrofit page.

Which materials is the system used for?

Sheet, coated steel and purlins on roll-forming and uncoiling lines. Exact figures are confirmed for each line.
