About us

Charger Mechatronic (甲子機電) is a Taiwanese industrial automation integrator founded in 2005. CMV is its company in Vietnam.

Company profile

Charger Mechatronic Co., Ltd. is an automation integrator supplying the best equipment, power control and programming for machinery. Our major items are AC/DC motors, servo drivers, servo motor controllers, encoders, PLC control, decentralized digital control, explosion-proof motors, system integration/design/revamping for the whole plant.

We provide power control system assisting in design, manufacture, installation & testing for customers. We also provide revamping projects for the control system of existing production lines. We aim to improve the efficiency and safety standard for production. We also cooperate with machinery provider on research & sales to ensure the best solution for our customers.

Facing the harsh reality under industrial globalization, we actively carry out the technology upgrading and transformation. We tend to be ahead of our competitors in respect of manufacturing technologies under the trend of industry 4.0. Our auto control technology helps collecting the front-end information of machinery, processing data analysis to improve the rate of mechanical movement and to enhance the supply chain management.

Charger Mechatronic constantly finances industry-academic collaboration projects to support talented students to be part of us in the future. We will enhance our competitiveness by retaining highly skilled employees, delving deep into technological innovation, being client centric and expanding our sales in the global market, expecting to be the leading icon in automation industry.
