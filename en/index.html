PLC control system integration, servo motor control and machinery. Specialists in cutting lines: flying shear, slitting, tube cutting and more

Charger Mechatronic Vietnam (CMV) supplies servo motors, drives, controllers and panels for flying shears, cut-to-length, slitting and tube lines, retrofits older lines, and supports them on site in Vietnam.

Request a quote See products

A Taiwanese automation integrator with local service in Ho Chi Minh City

Charger Mechatronic (甲子機電) has designed, built, installed and commissioned automatic control systems for manufacturers since 2005, and more than 100 of its cutting-line control systems have been running in Vietnam for over 15 years. To give existing and new customers higher-value, more convenient local service, it set up CMV, its first Vietnam branch, in Ho Chi Minh City in 2025.

CMV is the exclusive official distributor of NUSCO (Japan) in Vietnam.

The electrical control systems on Welltime (威泰鋼鐵) steel coil cutting lines are all built by Charger Mechatronic.

As of 2026, Charger Mechatronic has sold more than 300 flying shear systems.

What we do

PLC automatic control systems and line controls

Low-voltage switchboards, PLC control cabinets, MCC panels and controls for flying shear, cut-to-length, slitting and tube lines.

On-site technical service and line retrofits

Troubleshooting, repair and upgrade of PLC systems, motors and drives; drive replacement; flying shear retrofits.

Servo motors, drives and controllers

SVMF and SVMJ series servo motors, VFI-CG80 drives, CG multi-axis controllers.

Mitsubishi Heavy Industries generators (MGS series)

Charger Mechatronic is the MGS project partner confirmed by Mitsubishi Heavy Industries Engine System Asia.

AI energy management system

Behind-the-meter smart microgrid: forecasts generation and load, schedules storage automatically.

Agency for steel and paper processing machinery

Steel flying shear, slitting and flying cut-off lines: Welltime (威泰鋼鐵, Taiwan). Corrugated box and board printing machines: Sunrise Machinery (長聲工業, Taiwan).

Frequently asked questions

What does CMV do?

CMV supplies PLC systems, servo motors, drives, multi-axis controllers and control panels for all kinds of production lines, retrofits older lines and provides engineering support in Vietnam.

CMV provides technical support and repair for PLC systems, motors, drives and electrical controls on all kinds of line equipment

Flying shears, cut-to-length lines, slitting lines, tube cutting, and whole-plant control systems.
