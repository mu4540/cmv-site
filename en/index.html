Electrical control for steel coil processing lines

Charger Mechatronic Vietnam (CMV) supplies servo motors, drives, controllers and panels for flying shears, cut-to-length, slitting and tube lines, retrofits older lines, and supports them on site in Vietnam.

A Taiwanese automation integrator, now in Ho Chi Minh City

Charger Mechatronic (甲子機電) has designed, built, installed and commissioned automatic control systems for manufacturers since 2005. CMV is the group's company in Vietnam: contracts, invoices and engineers come from Ho Chi Minh City.

CMV is the exclusive official distributor of NUSCO (Japan) in Vietnam.

Rotary shear retrofit

±0.3±0.1mm

A typical retrofit: cut-length tolerance from over ±0.3 mm to within ±0.1 mm, output up 20% or more. Sound mechanical parts stay in place.

By 2014 Charger Mechatronic had sold 200 flying shear servo systems.
