Rotary shear retrofit for steel coil processing plants in Vietnam: CMV replaces the controller, servo motors, drives and length encoder and keeps the mechanical parts that are still sound. Who it is for

  • Coil service centers cutting sheet to length
  • Coated sheet and pipe mills with flying or rotary shears
  • Roll-forming and purlin machine builders who need flying-cut control
  • Lines with wide length tolerance, speed limited by the knife, or an old controller without spare parts

Frequently asked questions

Can older lines, including lines bought from China, be retrofitted?

Usually yes. CMV replaces the controller, servo motors, drives and length encoder and keeps the mechanical parts that are still sound. Feasibility is confirmed after reviewing photos, video and line data, or after a site survey.

How long does the line have to stop?

It depends on the line's condition and the scope. The downtime is stated in the quotation so the plant can plan production around it.

How is the tolerance after the retrofit accepted?

By measuring the length of a run of consecutive cut sheets at real operating speed. Sample size, method and acceptance criteria are agreed in the contract before work starts.

Why does a retrofit lower the electricity bill?

When the knife decelerates, the drive returns the braking energy to the power system for reuse instead of burning it as heat. Savings depend on each line's cut cycle and speed.

How is CMV related to NUSCO?

CMV is the exclusive official distributor of NUSCO (Japan) in Vietnam.
